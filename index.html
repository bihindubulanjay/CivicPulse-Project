The 4-Layer Zero-Fake Trust Engine

During disasters, recycled WhatsApp rumors and false tsunami alarms cause deadly stampedes. CivicPulse filters out false reports in milliseconds through four uncompromising hardware & AI checkpoints.

LAYER 01 • PROXIMITY

Haversine GPS Geofencing

Reports must originate within a strict physical radius (<800m) of Sri Lankan cell towers, GPS constellations, or LoRa repeaters. Overseas VPN or spoofed coordinates are flagged instantly.

haversine(device_coord, incident_node) < 0.8 km
LAYER 02 • INTEGRITY

Rust SHA-256 Edge Hashing

Every in-browser `LIVE REC` camera frame is hashed at capture time in WebAssembly/Rust. Prevents file tampering, recycled historical flood media (e.g., 2004 or 2016 archives), and deepfakes.

sha256::digest(raw_buffer) -> 9f84c2...
LAYER 03 • MULTIMODAL AI

Gemini Vision Auto-Quarantine

Google Gemini Vision analyzes the incident video stream against official Sri Lanka Dept. of Meteorology radar and hydrological gauges. Outlier panic claims are isolated into a quarantine sandbox.

gemini.verify(stream, sensor_context) >= 0.95
LAYER 04 • COMMUNITY HARMONIZATION

Local-First IndexedDB Store & Forward

Every hazard report captured during blackouts is immediately timestamped, cryptographically sealed with the device's public key, and saved into an encrypted browser SQLite/IndexedDB store.

Bluetooth Low Energy & LoRa Repeater Hops

When cellular towers flood or lose power, CivicPulse packets hop peer-to-peer across hiker smartphones, Sri Lanka Telecom LoRa gateways, and rural tea factory antennas until reaching an internet-connected node.

Atomic Re-Sync & Collision Resolution

Upon re-establishing uplink, batch reports execute a zero-conflict SHA-256 handshake. Duplicate reports from the same location merge automatically into a consensus certainty cluster.
